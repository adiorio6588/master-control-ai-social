Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Master Control Program ("Master Control", "we", "our", or "us") provides tools that help businesses manage social media comments, connected accounts, reply workflows, and related business communications.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to users of Master Control.

Information We Collect

We may collect account information such as your name, email address, organization information, and login credentials needed to access Master Control.

When you connect a supported social media account, Master Control may receive information made available through that platform's API, including Page or account identifiers, account names, posts, comments, comment identifiers, and other information necessary to provide the requested features.

We may also store replies, automation settings, business rules, and activity created while using Master Control.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate Master Control, connect authorized social media accounts, display comments, generate or manage suggested replies, publish approved responses, maintain account settings, improve reliability, and provide customer support.

We do not sell personal information.

Facebook and Meta Platform Data

When a user authorizes Master Control to access a Facebook Page or other Meta service, Master Control only accesses information permitted by the user and by the permissions granted through Meta.

Meta platform data is used only to provide the features requested by the authorized user, such as retrieving Page comments and publishing approved replies.

Artificial Intelligence

Master Control may use third-party artificial intelligence services to generate suggested responses or assist with business communication.

Information necessary to generate a response may be transmitted to those service providers in accordance with their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information stored by Master Control.

Access tokens and other sensitive credentials are intended to be stored securely and are not displayed publicly through the application.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account history, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the security of Master Control.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Master Control account and associated data by contacting us using the contact information below.

Users may also disconnect supported third-party platforms from Master Control, which prevents future access through that connection.

Third-Party Services

Master Control may integrate with third-party platforms such as Meta and artificial intelligence providers. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Master Control service evolves. The updated date shown at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
